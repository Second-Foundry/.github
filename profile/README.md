**We go deep with founders to validate, build, and ship products that work in the real world.**

2nd Foundry is a venture studio focused on execution. We work alongside you through the hard problems, from first concept to production.

## 🔨 The Forge

Products we've built and run in-house.

### [Labsistant](https://labsistant.com)
Lab inventory management software. A focused platform for teams that need to know what they have, where it is, and when it expires. Cuts waste and missed experiments without taking on a full LIMS or ELN.

### [mAIn Character](https://maincharacter.enterprises)
A playground for AI experiments. Playful, experimental ways AI can interact with the world as it becomes more present in everyday life, with less rigidity than formal product work.

### [Whetstone Network](https://whetstone.network)
Hone your plan in a 1-on-1 against our network of experts before you spend real time, money, and energy. Bring a decision you're stuck on, get it pressure-tested, and walk away with a clear call: ship, refine, or rethink.

### [BingeBonds](https://bingebonds.com)
Discover, rank, and share the media you love. A social entertainment platform for tracking TV, film, anime, games, books, and audiobooks, and finding what's next through friends and community rankings.

### [FM Assistant Manager AI](https://assman.ai)
AI Assistant Manager for Football Manager. Upload a screenshot of your tactics and get a breakdown of how your formation, roles, and instructions work together. Over 2,000 tactics reviewed since July 2025.

### [Vid2Sprite](https://vid2sprite.com)
Turn green screen videos into production-ready sprite sheets. A browser-based studio with chroma key tools, exact-frame capture, and clean PNG or sprite sheet export.
